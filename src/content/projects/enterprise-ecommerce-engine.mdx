## Problem

A rapidly growing retailer was struggling with a monolithic e-commerce platform that couldn't handle international traffic spikes or complex, region-specific fulfillment rules. Page load times were exceeding 4 seconds, leading to a high bounce rate and lost revenue.

<Callout type="warning">
  For every 100ms of latency, e-commerce conversion rates can drop by as much as 7%.
</Callout>

## Solution

I architected and implemented a modern headless commerce solution. By decoupling the frontend from the backend, we achieved ultra-fast performance while allowing for highly customized, localized shopping experiences.

<MetricCard 
  label="Performance Boost" 
  value="40%" 
  description="Improvement in checkout flow speed and overall page responsiveness." 
/>

## Architecture

The system utilizes a distributed microservices approach:
- **Frontend**: Next.js App Router with ISR (Incremental Static Regeneration) for lightning-fast product pages.
- **Backend**: FastAPI (Python) handles the core business logic, inventory, and order management.
- **Payments**: A robust Stripe integration that supports multi-currency and localized tax compliance.
- **Infrastructure**: Containerized with Docker and deployed on AWS for seamless scaling.

<Callout type="idea">
  Implementing a globally distributed CDN for static assets and product data reduced time-to-first-byte (TTFB) by 60% for international users.
</Callout>

## Tech Stack

- **Framework**: Next.js for a seamless, SEO-friendly user interface.
- **API**: FastAPI for its high-performance asynchronous capabilities.
- **Payment**: Stripe for secure and flexible global transactions.
- **Database**: PostgreSQL for reliable and structured data persistence.

## Results

- **40% faster checkout flow**, significantly improving user satisfaction.
- **25% reduction in cart abandonment** within the first three months of launch.
- **Successful expansion** into three new international markets with localized fulfillment logic.

<Callout type="success">
  The new platform successfully handled a 300% traffic surge during Black Friday without any downtime or performance degradation.
</Callout>
